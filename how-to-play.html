How to play Entangled Chaos

Watch the field, choose your moment and turn a small spark into a chain reaction.

Check the objective before tapping

Some rounds ask you to clear every target. Others require an arrow sequence, or both an arrow sequence and a cleared field. Read the objective and remaining tap allowance first. Black Holes and Photons are not ordinary clearable targets.

Create a spark

Tap empty space in the arena to create a growing spark. If it reaches an eligible particle, that particle reacts and can trigger others. Each accepted tap spends part of your allowance. A tap that misses still counts.

Follow moving particles

Particles keep moving while you plan. Look for an opening where the spark can connect a group. You can use another tap while a chain is still running, but save it for a useful position rather than tapping repeatedly.

Watch the remaining targets and star goals

Your live score reflects the chain, triggered particles, tap timing and time remaining. Check the displayed star thresholds. Revisit a round to try a different approach and improve your result.

Learn through the in-game guides

The first round introduces taps, targets and scoring. New particle introductions explain their special interactions. Pause to read them and consult the particle guide when an unfamiliar body changes the field.

Can I play offline?

You can play offline and save progress on your device. Downloads, online rankings, purchases, optional rewarded ads and cloud features need connectivity where available. Platform gamer profiles and private cloud accounts are separate.
