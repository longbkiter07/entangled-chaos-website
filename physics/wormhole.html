THE SCIENCE BEHIND THE GAME

Wormholes: a mathematical possibility, not a discovered shortcut

A route can be long along a surface yet short through a different connection. That picture makes wormholes appealing in science fiction. In physics, however, the question is whether spacetime could contain such a connection at all, not whether someone has found a usable portal.

Are wormholes real?

Wormhole geometries arise in work on general relativity. There is no observational evidence that a traversable wormhole exists, and a mathematical possibility is not a demonstrated way to transport a spacecraft or particle. Creating and keeping such a passage open remain speculative ideas. NASA: Wormholes and faster-than-light theories ↗

Where the game takes a different path

The game makes the speculative idea concrete: two linked portals move a body between locations. Their velocity preservation, explosions and special treatment of Photons are invented rules, not experimental evidence for wormhole travel.

A shortcut for your imagination
