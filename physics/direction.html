THE SCIENCE BEHIND THE GAME

Direction and momentum: why an arrow matters

Two objects can move at the same speed and still head toward very different futures. A speedometer alone does not tell you where a car is going. Physics therefore needs quantities that describe both the size of an effect and its direction.

What does direction add to motion?

Velocity, force and momentum are vectors. A net force changes motion, and the direction of that force matters. Even at a steady speed, changing direction means changing velocity. NASA uses these ideas to explain how forces guide objects through air and space. NASA: Newton’s laws of motion ↗

Where the game takes a different path

Direction is not a real particle species. The arrows in Entangled Chaos are symbols in an objective sequence, not a measurement of velocity. Following their order is a logic challenge rather than a simulation of a conservation law.

Read the symbol before acting

A particle can travel one way while its badge asks for a different action. The puzzle encourages you to separate a rule shown by a symbol from the physical motion you see on the screen.

Direction in Entangled Chaos
