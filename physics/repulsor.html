THE SCIENCE BEHIND THE GAME

Repulsion: the invisible push between charges

Try bringing the same poles of two magnets together: you feel a push before they touch. At a smaller scale, electrically charged objects also exert forces across space. A force can change motion without a collision between visible surfaces.

Can a force push without contact?

Objects with matching electric charges repel; opposite charges attract. Electric and magnetic fields are linked parts of electromagnetism. This interaction acts on charged matter, and its effects depend on the situation rather than on one universal push applied to everything nearby. DOE: The electromagnetic force ↗

Where the game takes a different path

Repulsor is a game invention, not a discovered particle. Its circular shove, range changes and species-specific exceptions are puzzle rules. They should not be read as a model of electrostatics or a practical anti-gravity machine.

From force to formation

The interesting puzzle is not always what explodes next. A push can change the geometry of the field, leaving you to reconsider a later tap. It is a playful reminder that interactions can rearrange a system before they release energy.

Repulsor in Entangled Chaos

When triggered, releases a field that pushes nearby bodies away instead of igniting them. Use the expanding field to open space or gather a useful formation elsewhere. Neutrinos ignore it; Black Holes feel a weaker push. Repulsor encounters can shrink its range.
