THE SCIENCE BEHIND THE GAME

Black holes: the boundary light cannot cross outward

A star can end its life leaving a black hole. From far away it exerts gravity like any other object with the same mass, but close in there is a boundary that changes the rules of escape. Cross the event horizon inward and no outward light signal can return to a distant observer.

What is an event horizon?

A black hole is a region of spacetime, not a hole in a solid surface. The bright material often pictured around one is outside the horizon. We learn about black holes through their effects on nearby matter and light; the invisible boundary itself is not a glowing shell. NASA: Black holes ↗

Where the game takes a different path

Orange, red and green protection stages are game signals. Real black holes do not lose a life when hit by a blast, gain a protective Photon shield, or collapse because a player touches them. Absorbing light adds energy rather than a magical armour layer.

The story behind the hazard

The game borrows the idea of a boundary with serious consequences. It turns that idea into something you can read and plan around, while real black-hole research asks what spacetime does under extreme conditions.

Black Hole in Entangled Chaos
