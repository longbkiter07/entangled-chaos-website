ACCOUNT AND DATA REQUESTS

Delete your Entangled Chaos data.

Use the in-app deletion flow in the latest Entangled Chaos version. It works for guests and signed-in game accounts; no email request is needed.

Delete your game account and data

  1. Open Account from the home screen.
  2. Select Delete game account and data.
  3. Read the permanent-deletion warning, then select Delete permanently.
  4. Keep the game connected while deletion completes. If the connection fails, the request is saved: reopen the game and choose Try again. The game confirms completion before starting a new guest profile.

This permanently removes the game's progress, submitted scores, app achievement records, wallet balances, inventory and leaderboard profile. It does not delete your Apple or Google account. iOS also resets this game’s linked Game Center achievements; Google Play achievement history is managed separately by Google. Anonymous purchase replay-prevention records and hashed deleted-owner markers are retained to prevent duplicate credits and stale uploads. A shared sign-in identity used by another LNTT game is retained for that game.

Remove local game data

On Android, clear the game's app storage in your device settings. On iOS, delete the app using Delete App; offloading it preserves its documents. Removing local storage does not delete server records or any device backups. Account sync may restore cloud progress unless those records have also been deleted.

Local data includes progress, inventory, diamond balance, language and sound preferences. Cloud records, when present, include your provider account identifier, round results, stars, scores, furthest progress achievements, wallet balances, item operations and leaderboard profile photo URL.

Deletion requests concern data controlled by Entangled Chaos. Google, app stores and hosting providers handle their own records under their respective policies. See our privacy notice.
