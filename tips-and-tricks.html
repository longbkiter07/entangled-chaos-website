Entangled Chaos tips and tricks

A good reaction starts before the first tap. Read the movement and plan for what happens next.

Wait for a useful connection

A group that looks separated now may drift together. Place your spark where particles are going, while watching the round timer and hazards. Do not assume the same timing works on every attempt.

Save a tap for isolated targets

Look for bodies the first chain may miss. Keep a remaining tap for a gap you can actually reach, and follow any arrow sequence in its displayed order.

Treat Black Holes as hazards

Do not try to clear a Black Hole like an ordinary target. Its protection state changes what a blast will do. Follow the in-game guide and plan where secondary explosions will travel, not just where your spark begins.

Watch what a Comet starts

A Comet can help connect the field, but can also ignite the wrong particle before you act. Watch its approach and contribute to the round yourself; an automatic clear without player contribution is not a successful player clear.

Check both ends of a pair

An Entangled partner can carry a reaction across the arena. Before triggering one, inspect the other end for useful targets and nearby hazards. Wormholes create a different opportunity: they transport moving bodies between linked positions.

Use help deliberately

Orbit Scan helps you inspect the field; Horizon Shield protects against Black Hole danger. Extra Tap can add a charge within the round limit. Items are optional tools, not proof that every difficult round has an item-free solution.

Learn from an unsuccessful attempt

Notice the first event that broke your plan: a missed spark, a moving survivor, an incorrect arrow or a dangerous secondary blast. Change one part of your timing or placement and try again.
